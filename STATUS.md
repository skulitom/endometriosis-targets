## Early observations (descriptive; not replication results)

- **One cohort dominates**: 186 of 305 primary claims rely on FinnGen alone.
- **Repeat publication**: Tao 2024 and Ling 2025 report near-identical estimates for FSHB and EPHB4 from the same UKB-PPP × FinnGen R10 data.
- **RSPO3 direction conflict persists after verification**: 6 claims from UKB-PPP, deCODE and Zheng 2020 proteins report higher RSPO3 as risk-increasing; the one from INTERVAL SomaScan proteins (Guo 2024 preprint, FinnGen R9) reports OR 0.753.
- **Source errors are common enough to matter**: 8 of 45 claim-bearing papers have at least one internal contradiction affecting a claim.
- **Most claims cannot be reproduced from what was published**: 177 of 305 primary claims (58%) give no per-variant effect sizes anywhere in the paper or supplements. Of the 15 rebuilt from a paper's own stated rule, only 4 give the paper's instrument count.

## Gate B state

Checksums, builds, positive controls and one reproduction are done and passed. Still open before the freeze:
- **Power**: Pan-UKBB has 4,354 cases, so many single-claim replications there will be inconclusive. Power per claim is computed at Gate C as the protocol says; nothing about claimed genes has been looked at.
- **Rahmioglu 2023 overlap** (resolved 2026-10-31 from Supplementary Table 1, which Artem downloaded; checksum in `data/manual/*.provenance.json`): FinnGen is **not** one of its 24 datasets. A Supplementary Table 20 footnote says FinnGen contributed only sub-phenotype results, and the case counts add up exactly to the public file's 21,779 European cases. The public file does contain UK Biobank, Estonian Biobank, deCODE, BioBank Japan and the Northern Finland Birth Cohort (NFBC: 167 cases, 5,235 controls). NFBC is a FinnGen legacy cohort, so the two share those participants: 0.8% of Rahmioglu's effective sample, estimated correlation between estimates about 0.009.
- **Decision (Artem, 2026-10-31)**: the NFBC overlap is accepted. Results resting on it are labelled "near-independent (NFBC overlap)", and the strictly independent estimate is also reported where one exists. As a result, the 98 claims found in FinnGen alone are re-tested in Rahmioglu 2023 (23,492 cases) instead of only Pan-UKBB (4,354), and 26 claims found in Rahmioglu 2023 become testable in FinnGen R12 (20,190 cases, from the R12 manifest).
